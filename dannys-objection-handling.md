# Danny's Bar & Grill — Objection Handling Cheat Sheet

**The offer:** $750 one-time to build & launch the site. Modifications afterward
are billed at **$50/hour** — no monthly subscription, no lock-in.

**The site is already live:** https://dannys-bar-grill-effingham.netlify.app
**Them:** Danny's Bar & Grill · 7722 US-45, Effingham, IL 62401 · (217) 536-5434

**Golden rules**
- Don't argue. Agree first ("Totally fair"), *then* answer.
- The site is already built and live — lead with that. They're not buying an
  idea, they're deciding whether to keep something real.
- After you answer an objection, ask a small yes-question to keep it moving.
- Silence is your friend. Say your line, then stop talking.

---

## 💰 "$750 is too much / too expensive"

> "I hear you — it's a real number. Here's how I'd think about it: design shops
> charge $3,000–$6,000 for exactly this, and it's a one-time cost, not a
> subscription. It's already built with your real photos, full menu, and the
> Qik Sak drive-up front and center. One extra table on a Friday fish fry night
> a couple times and it's paid for itself. Want to walk through it on your phone
> real quick so you can see what the $750 actually gets you?"

**Follow-ups:**
- If truly budget-tight: "I can split it — half to launch, half in 30 days.
  Does that make it easier?"
- "The expensive website is the one that sends customers to a dead page — which
  is what's happening on your current one right now."

---

## 🤔 "I already have a website"

> "You do — and I actually looked at it. That's part of why I reached out: the
> online-order button on it goes to a dead page right now, so anyone trying to
> order is hitting a wall. Mine loads fast on a phone, has live hours, one-tap
> call and directions, and the full menu. Pull them up side by side on your
> phone — if yours does everything mine does, I'll leave you alone. Fair?"

**Follow-ups:**
- "Most folks look you up on their phone in the parking lot. Is your current one
  built for that, or does it make them pinch and zoom?"
- "You keep your same web address — I just put something better behind it."

---

## ⏳ "I need to think about it / talk to my partner"

> "Makes total sense — it's your business, you shouldn't rush it. The site's not
> going anywhere; it's live right now, so the best thing you can do is send them
> the link and look at it together tonight: dannys-bar-grill-effingham.netlify.app.
> What's the one thing you'd need to feel good about it? Let me answer that now so
> you're not guessing later."

**Follow-ups:**
- "Want me to hold the $750 launch price through the weekend so the decision
  isn't about the clock?"
- "Is it the money, the timing, or you just want to see it first? Any of those
  I can help with."

---

## 🔒 "I don't want to be locked into a monthly fee"

> "Good — because there isn't one. It's $750 once, and that's it. If you ever
> want changes down the road — new menu prices, a holiday post, a photo swap —
> that's just $50 an hour, and only if and when you actually want something
> changed. Most months that's zero. You're never paying to keep the lights on."

**Follow-ups:**
- "No contract, no auto-charge. You own the result."
- "Think of the $50/hour like calling a handyman — only when there's actually
  something to do."

---

## 😐 "We don't really need a website / word of mouth is fine"

> "Word of mouth is your best asset — I'd never argue that. But here's the thing:
> when someone hears 'you gotta try Danny's,' the first thing they do is Google
> you from their phone. Right now that's where the dead order-button and the
> hard-to-read page live. A good site doesn't replace word of mouth — it catches
> it. You've got a 4.5-star rating; the site puts that, your hours, and a call
> button one tap away. That's just turning talk into people through the door."

**Follow-ups:**
- "Friday fish fry, karaoke, Saturday bands — how does someone new find out
  those are happening tonight?"
- "It's already done. The only question is whether you want it working for you
  or sitting there unused."

---

## 🛠️ "Who maintains it? What if I want changes later?"

> "That's me. I host it and keep it running — you don't touch a thing. If you
> want a change — updated prices, a new photo, an event added — you text me, and
> it's $50 an hour, usually a quick job. No ticket system, no waiting on some big
> company. You call the guy who built it."

**Follow-ups:**
- "Small stuff like a price or a photo is often 15–20 minutes — I'll always tell
  you the time before I start."
- "You'll never log into anything or fight with software. That's the whole point."

---

## 🚪 Closing lines (pick one)

- "Everything's built and live — want me to point it at your real web address so
  it's officially yours? I can have that done in a couple days."
- "How about this: come look at it on your phone with me for two minutes. If you
  like it, we do the $750 and it's live on your address this week."
- "What would need to be true for this to be an easy yes?"
