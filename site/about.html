My story

It started with a borrowed camera and a very patient family.

I picked up my first "real" camera in college, mostly to photograph friends and the foggy Oregon coast. What began as a way to remember small moments quietly became the thing I couldn't stop doing. A decade and a few hundred stories later, it's still the same feeling every time — the privilege of being handed someone's most important day.

I'm drawn to natural light, honest emotion, and the in-between frames most people never think to look for — the deep breath, the shared glance, the tears someone tries to blink away. Those are the ones you'll treasure long after the posed shots.

What I believe

A few things that shape every session

Light over everything

I shoot with what's already there — window light, golden hour, the soft grey of an Oregon morning. It's what gives the work its timeless, unforced glow.

Comfort first

Nervous in front of the camera? Same, honestly. I direct just enough to make you feel steady, then get out of the way so the real you can show up.

Made to last

Trends fade; your photos shouldn't. I edit for a natural, archival look you'll love just as much in twenty years as you do today.
