# Your website editor — a quick guide

Hi Nikki! You can change your website yourself, anytime, without touching any
code. Here's how.

## Getting in

1. Go to **your-site-address/admin** (for example, `nicolefieldsphotography.net/admin`).
2. Type your **password** and click **Unlock editor**.
   (Your developer sets this password — it's the site's `ADMIN_KEY`.)

## Editing

Everything is grouped into panels. Click a panel to open it:

- **Business & story** — your name, tagline, the intro on the homepage, and your
  longer "about me" story.
- **Location & area** — your city, state, and the "available across Illinois" line.
- **Contact & social** — your email, phone, Instagram, and Facebook. Anything you
  leave blank simply won't show on the site.
- **Services** — everything you photograph. You can:
  - edit a name, price, length, or description,
  - add a service with **＋ Add a service**,
  - add a whole new group with **＋ Add a service group**,
  - remove one with the ✕ / **Remove group** buttons (it asks you to confirm).
  - Leave a price blank and the site shows **"Inquire"**.
- **Show / hide sections** — switches for your business hours, Google rating, and
  the reviews section.

As you type, you'll see **"Unsaved changes"** at the top.

## Saving

Click **Save changes** (top right). That's it — your live website updates right
away. Visitors see the new version immediately.

## Backing up (recommended before big changes)

Open **Backup & restore**:

- **Export content** downloads a file with everything on your site. Keep it safe.
- **Import content** loads a file back in (then click Save).
- **Discard local changes** throws away unsaved edits and reloads the last saved
  version.

## Good to know

- Your edits are saved on the website's server, so they show for everyone.
- If you close the tab mid-edit without saving, your changes are kept on that same
  computer/browser until you come back — but they aren't live until you **Save**.
- **Photos** aren't editable here yet — send new galleries to your developer and
  they'll add them.
- Forgot your password, or want to change it? Ask your developer (it's the
  `ADMIN_KEY` setting).

Click **Lock** (top right) when you're done, especially on a shared computer.
