The approach

Laid-back, unhurried, and completely you.

You shouldn't have to perform for your own photos. I take it moment by moment and go with the flow — kids melt down, babies need snacks, and that's all part of it.

Photography is my form of personal expression, and my family is my whole heart — my husband and our two boys keep life joyful and a little chaotic in the best way. That's exactly the kind of real, in-between moment I love to capture for other families.
