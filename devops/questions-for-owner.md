invented to fill these. Most can be answered directly in the **`/admin`** editor.

   The single most important item before launch.
2. **Contact details** — confirm the real **email**, **phone**, **Instagram**,

   found online may belong to a *different* Nicole Fields — left off until you
   confirm.) Add them in `/admin` → Contact.
3. **Location wording** — currently "Effingham, IL · available across Illinois."
   Adjust if you want a different service area.
4. **Pricing** — starting prices/ranges per service (families, newborn, seniors,
   engagements, weddings, headshots, sports, framing). Shown as "Inquire" until set.
5. **Business hours** — hidden until provided (toggle on in `/admin` → Show/Hide).
6. **Google Business Profile** — place ID + whether to show the live rating badge
   and turn on the reviews section (off by default; nothing auto-publishes).
7. **Testimonials** — any client quotes to feature (shown without Google attribution)?
8. **Brand** — logo file, colors, fonts. Current coral + slate liquid-glass palette
   is a provisional proposal; confirm or redirect.
9. **Favicon** — a monogram is generated; OK to keep, or supply a mark?
10. **Admin password** — choose the value for `ADMIN_KEY` (set in Netlify env).
11. **Contact form delivery** — confirm the inbox inquiries should reach
